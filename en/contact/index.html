FAQ

Questions & answers

You can ask me specific questions any time via the form above — or later via Instagram and Telegram DM.

How long does the 1:1 coaching last?
The coaching is not a fixed number of hours but a 1:1 mentoring over several weeks. Exactly how long depends on your current level of knowledge — in the introductory call we put together an individual learning plan for that.
How long are the sessions in the coaching?
As long as the topic requires: each session is planned around a specific topic, not about sitting out hours. We schedule the sessions individually — the coaching should fit into your daily life, not the other way around.
Is there a discount on the 1:1 coaching?
No — the price is calculated fairly and is the same for everyone. More important than the price is whether we are a good fit: that is exactly what the introductory call is for.
Do you ever show payouts?
I share documented trades with entry, stop-loss and reasoning — for educational purposes. You will not see payout screenshots used as marketing here: past results are no guarantee of future performance, and credibility matters more to me than glossy marketing.
Is there an age limit?
Yes, the offer is aimed at adults (18+). Brokers and prop firms require you to be of legal age anyway.
Why do you sell courses at all?
Fair question. Coaching is my “on top” — it pays for the family holiday and I simply enjoy it, because I spent years desperately searching for exactly this kind of shortcut myself. That is why it stays honest: no promises of returns.
Do I need prior experience to start trading?
No. The ICT Mentorship starts from zero — understanding the market, chart analysis, terminology — and then goes deeper step by step. With prior experience, you can jump straight into the advanced concepts or Patrick’s own approach.
Is a mentorship enough to become profitable?
The mentorships give you the complete toolkit: ICT or the Precision System, risk management and the community. Whether you become profitable depends on your execution, discipline and risk control — no serious offer can or will guarantee profitability.
Why should I book the coaching with you and not with a trading guru?
You don’t have to — take your time and compare. With me you get fixed trading hours, strict risk management and documented trades instead of Lambos and promises of returns. That is exactly what the introductory call is for: seeing whether we are a good fit.

More questions? Write to me — via the form above or directly by email.
