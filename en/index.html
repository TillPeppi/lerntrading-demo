FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior knowledge?
No. The ICT Mentorship starts from zero — understanding the market, chart analysis, terminology — and then explains ICT step by step in depth. If you already have experience, you can jump straight into the advanced concepts or Patrick’s own approach.
How much time do I need per day?
The Precision System works with two fixed time windows (8:00–9:30 AM and 3:30–5:00 PM CET) with around 30 minutes of active work. You don’t need both sessions — one is enough to trade according to plan.
Can I take on a prop-firm challenge with the system?
The system teaches exactly the craft prop firms want to see: strict risk management, rule adherence, and documentation. I specifically prepare coaching participants for it — but I cannot and will not guarantee that you’ll pass: the outcome depends on your execution.
What do I get in the community?
Exchange with other traders, daily trade ideas with reasoning, recaps, Q & A, and trade reviews. Full community access is available as a monthly subscription (already included in the mentorships).
Is this investment advice?
No. LernTrading.de is purely an educational offering. There are no individual investment recommendations, no asset management, and no profit promises. Shared trades document my own decisions for educational purposes — every trading decision is yours alone. Details in the risk disclosure.
Why do you sell courses at all?
Fair question. Coaching is my "on top" — it pays for the family vacation and I simply enjoy it, because I spent years desperately searching for exactly this kind of shortcut myself. That’s why it stays honest: no promises of returns.
