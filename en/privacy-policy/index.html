On the offer page, you can voluntarily join a waitlist to be notified by email as soon as our offers become available. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO), which you give by submitting the form and ticking the checkbox.

Your sign-up is delivered to our own mailboxes by email via Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., USA; transfers to the USA are governed by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses) and processed there. We use your address exclusively to inform you once, or when available, about the launch of the offers; it is not shared with any other third parties or used for any other advertising.

You can revoke your consent at any time with effect for the future — an informal email to klose.patrick0599@gmail.com is sufficient. We delete your email address as soon as you revoke your consent or the purpose (notification about the launch) no longer applies.

In addition, we count anonymously and in aggregate how often the waitlist button is clicked and how often the form is submitted. This counting takes place server-side without cookies; no personal data (in particular no IP addresses) is stored — only counters are incremented. It is not possible to draw conclusions about individual persons.
